Sample: Lisbon holiday-rental register (RNAL)

Checked 23 Oct 2026 with a browser.

The register shows who runs each rental

Every RNAL record lists the operator's role (owner, tenant or borrower), tax number, name, email and often a phone number. Portuguese law requires this to be published (DL 128/2014, art. 10). This is the most direct route to owner contacts that we found.
